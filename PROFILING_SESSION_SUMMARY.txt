================================================================================
MICRO-BENCHMARK RESULTS
================================================================================

Tree: 10M items, Capacity: 128

Operation Costs:
  Iterator creation only:              3.36ns   ✓ Optimal
  Iterator + first next():             704ns    🔥 Primary hotspot
  Per-item (10 item batches):          20.39ns  (high setup overhead)
  Per-item (100 item batches):         6.13ns   ✓ Good amortization
  Per-item (within leaf):              2.86ns   ✓ Excellent
  Per-item (cross leaf):               4.18ns   ✓ Very good
  Per-item (bounded range):            9.23ns   (bound check overhead)

Analysis:
  - Setup overhead (704ns) is 94.8% of cost for small iterations
  - Amortization works well: 100+ items reduces to ~7ns/item overhead
  - Leaf boundary crossing is very efficient (only 1.31ns extra)
  - Within-leaf iteration is memory-bandwidth limited (2.86ns)
